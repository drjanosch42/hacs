This blueprint is the formal ground truth of the project: statements of the mathematics of the contract [C] that have
been, or are planned to be, verified in Lean~4 with mathlib, and the certificate checkers that tie the C++ computations
to them. Each statement names its Lean declarations and its status: proved, proved modulo a named literature input,
stated, or planned; a register lists the literature inputs. A statement mirroring one of [C] keeps [C]'s label verbatim.
It covers the conventions (symmetric power, pairing, Hecke normalisation, Bernoulli numbers, nebentypus); the
correspondence datum and the boundary cosets; the bulk complex on the seven-vertex model; double cosets and Hecke
operators on resolutions; the Hecke ring and level $N$; the pair, the cone and its Hecke action; the projective
reduction; saturation and the Hecke algebra modulo powers of $\ell$; the Moerdijk--Pronk model; and the integral Hecke
structure, beginning with Ramanujan's $691$.

The blueprint is \emph{not yet binding}: [C] remains the contract. For a clause of [C] marked as formally verified, its
row here is the statement of record, and a discrepancy is an erratum of [C]. For a clause resting on a literature input
this holds only while its locators stand verified, the recorded repair of \texttt{thm:classical} stays in place, and
the gates are green at every close.
